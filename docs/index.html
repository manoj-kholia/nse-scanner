Selection, not a tested system. The research this follows found the opening-range breakout itself returned about 3% a year on its own, and about 42% when restricted to the stocks opening on abnormal volume — the shortlist is the part with evidence behind it, not the entry. Free intraday data reaches back roughly 60 days, which is far too little to backtest, so none of this has been validated on history. SEBI’s July 2024 study found 71% of individual intraday traders in the equity cash segment lost money, and that loss-makers paid costs worth an extra 57% of their losses — which is why every row carries its own break-even. Paper-trade it first.
How this fits together. update_nse_data.py pulls prices → this page screens and ranks them → click any symbol to open its daily chart in TradingView with your Cup & Handle script.
Screening rules: above the 200 DMA and the 50 DMA, within the chosen distance of the 20-week high, and liquid enough to trade. Volume is shown in lakh (L) and crore (Cr).
Not investment advice. The ranking score is a sorting convenience, not a signal — confirm the base on the chart before acting.
